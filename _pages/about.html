Ziyue (Darcy) Zheng

Master's student · Johns Hopkins University

Hi! I'm a Master's student at Johns Hopkins University, working on robotics and perception.

I am interested in how robots decide where to look and how to move to better understand their surroundings. My current work focuses on active perception and 3D scene reconstruction. I am also interested in event-based vision and robot learning.

Recently, I worked on TRACE, a trajectory planning method for active scene reconstruction with real robots.
